Symptoms

Kya aap inmein se kisi bhi symptom se pareshan hain?

Aise chronic health symptoms jo aapki daily life ko kharab kar rahe hain:

Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS)

Din bhar bahut zyada thakaan rehna aur achhi neend lene ke baad bhi body mein energy feel na hona.

Fibromyalgia

Poori body mein dard, muscles mein akadand (stiffness) aur sirf touch karne par bhi dard feel hona.

Chronic Pain

Muscles aur joints mein lagataar dard rehna, jiska koi saaf physical reason samajh nahi aata.

Brain Fog

Focus na kar pana, mental laziness, confusion feel hona aur baatein bhool jana.

IBS aur Digestion Problems

Pet mein gas, acidity, heaviness, constipation (kabz) ya loose motions jaisi problems lagataar bane rehna.

Long COVID

Infection se theek hone ke mahino baad bhi thakaan, saans phoolna aur kamzori feel hona.

Anxiety aur Stress

Mind ka hamesha ashhant rehna, ghabrahat hona aur health ko lekar lagataar negative thoughts aana.

Sleep Problems

Sone mein dikkat hona, raat ko baar-baar neend tootna aur subah uthne par freshness feel na hona.
